About GFM

GFM is a civil engineering consulting firm specializing in Geo-Engineering and Construction Digitalization. We enhance clients in professional and geotechnical engineering. GFM provides expertise in landslide assessment and mitigation, slope stability analysis, geotechnical investigation, ground improvement, and infrastructure planning that prioritizes safety, effectiveness, and sustainability. We believe that every project presents unique challenges, and our innovative solutions are tailored to the specific considerations of each client.

In addition to geotechnical engineering services, GFM supports clients' digital transformation in the construction industry. Building Information Modeling (BIM) services include BIM model development, multidisciplinary design coordination, project data management, and implementation to improve project efficiency and quality. GFM also offers Green Building solutions through Building Energy Analysis and Lighting Design services to support energy-efficient and environmentally responsible development.

As a customer-oriented company, GFM emphasizes responsive communication, collaboration, and professionalism throughout every stage of a project. We believe that successful project outcomes are achieved not only through strong technical capability, but also through a deep understanding of client needs and the ability to deliver practical solutions with long-term value.
