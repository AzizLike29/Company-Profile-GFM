LHMS Release v1.5 Now Available

We're excited to announce the release of LHMS v1.5, packed with powerful new features and improvements designed to enhance your slope management experience.

  • ✓ Advanced slope visualization with 3D rendering
  • ✓ Real-time hazard mapping and analytics
  • ✓ Streamlined inspection workflow with mobile support
  • ✓ Enhanced WebGIS with satellite imagery integration

⚡ Improvements

📰 News May 10, 2026

GFM Consult Launches WebGIS Dashboard

GFM Consult is proud to unveil the next generation of spatial slope monitoring with our new WebGIS Dashboard, now integrated directly into LHMS.

The WebGIS dashboard provides real-time slope monitoring and spatial visualization for comprehensive asset management. Users can now:

  • → Visualize slopes on interactive maps with real-time hazard overlays
  • → Export maps and reports in multiple formats
  • → Integrate satellite imagery and terrain models
  • → Support for collaborative analysis and decision-making

📰 News May 5, 2026

Partnership Announcement: Infrastructure Authority

LHMS has been officially selected as the slope management system for the national infrastructure monitoring program.

This strategic partnership represents a major endorsement of LHMS technology and its effectiveness in managing complex slope networks. The national infrastructure program will use LHMS to monitor and manage slopes across highways, railways, and water management infrastructure.

Key benefits of this partnership:

  • ✓ Deployment across 5,000+ slope assets nationwide
  • ✓ Real-time monitoring and decision support
  • ✓ Integration with national disaster management systems
  • ✓ Training and support for 500+ government personnel
